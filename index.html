About Me

Hello, I'm Brittany! Senior Product Designer at Amazon and cofounder of slow•tech studio.

Currently — I design for everyone's favorite part of the shopping experience: tracking and managing orders mid-flight. Think modifying payment, changing the address, or rescheduling delivery to a better day.

Formerly — I’ve worked on Amazon Flex, the last mile logistics tool used by over ~2 million delivery transporters on foot, bikes, trucks, and motorcycles across the globe, and Amazon Chime, the video conferencing platform used by ~1.6 million corporate employees between 2017 and 2026 (RIP).

I click best with teammates who fall in love with real problems, speak plainly, think critically, and invest in each other’s success. Starting my design career at Amazon meant diving in feet-first with many a cross functional team, and gaining exposure to enterprise SASS, supply chain, and consumer-facing software. I’ve learned on the job how to advocate for users of communication platforms, the gig-economy marketplace, and, of course, online retail.

slow•tech studio is an independent design collective I co-founded with Sydney T Nguyễn. We speak and write about reclaiming emergent technology to support human flourishing at scale.

In another life — I was as a food photographer/custom picture framer. Still grateful to get paid making useful things. ✨
